FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. Papers, timer, challenges and community are free to use.

How does XP work?

You earn XP for time on the site, finished focus sessions, correct daily answers, roadmap topics, posts and comments. Levels unlock new titles. XP is saved in your own browser.

Can I request a paper or topic?

Post it on the Community board or comment on a video on YouTube.

Who posts the papers?

Only the site owner. The papers come from the official competition organisers' public releases.
